# Manufacturing Operations & Predictive Maintenance Analytics

### Turning Industrial Data into Business Intelligence

**Project:** Daikibo Industrials – Deloitte Australia Data Analytics Job Simulation  
**Focus:** Data Analytics | Business Intelligence | Predictive Maintenance

---

## 📌 Project Overview

An end-to-end analytics project focused on identifying manufacturing inefficiencies, predicting equipment failures, and translating operational data into actionable business insights.

Using **160,704 industrial telemetry records** across four manufacturing facilities, this project analyses equipment reliability, production downtime, financial losses, and maintenance risks.

The objective is to help business leaders make data-driven operational and financial decisions.

## 📊 Key Business Insights

- Analysed **160,704 sensor records** across 36 manufacturing assets.
- Identified 29 equipment failure incidents and 1,030 minutes of total downtime.
- Discovered that the morning shift accounted for **58.25% of total downtime**.
- Identified Shenzhen as the facility with the highest financial losses.
- Developed predictive maintenance models, achieving up to **0.970 ROC-AUC**.
- Estimated potential annual savings of **$247,426** through predictive maintenance and operational improvements.

*Financial savings are modelled projections, not realized business results.*

## 📈 Power BI Dashboard

Developed a four-page Power BI dashboard to help stakeholders monitor performance and identify operational risks.

1. **Executive Overview** – Global KPIs and manufacturing performance.
2. **Factory Performance** – Downtime analysis and facility comparisons.
3. **Machine Reliability** – Equipment failures and maintenance metrics.
4. **Financial Impact** – Cost analysis and interactive ROI scenarios.
